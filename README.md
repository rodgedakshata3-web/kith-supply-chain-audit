👟 KITH Streetwear & Lifestyle Inventory Audit & Supply Chain Simulation

## 📌 Project Overview & Purpose
During high-demand streetwear drops and limited-edition seasonal releases, apparel and lifestyle brands like **KITH** experience massive spikes in web traffic, rapid sell-through velocities, and severe inventory stockout pressures. 

The purpose of this project is to build an **enterprise-scale data audit and simulation pipeline** modeling inventory variants across high-demand streetwear categories (Hoodies, Footwear, Outerwear, Tees, Accessories). The goal is to audit stockout risks, regional fulfillment bottlenecks, and supply chain health under high-stress drop conditions.

---

## 🏗️ Architecture & Deliverables
1. **Python Audit Simulation Engine (`kith_audit_script.py`):** Generates structured inventory records incorporating category matrices, stock statuses, return rates, and fulfillment metrics.
2. **Multi-Tab Excel Inventory Model (`KITH INVENTORY MODEL.xlsx`):** A professional workbook structured into core audit tabs:
   * **Executive_Summary:** Category-level performance aggregations tracking critical stockout rates and sell-through velocities.
   * **Regional_Telemetry:** Operational breakdown across fulfillment hubs and distribution nodes.
   * **Strategic_Solutions:** An executive matrix outlining inventory optimization solutions and agile replenishment frameworks.
   * **Raw_Audit_Data:** The complete underlying telemetry dataset.

---

## 🔍 Key Findings & Analytical Insights
* **Stockout Vulnerability:** High-demand streetwear releases experience severe inventory depletion under peak drop traffic, highlighting the challenge of balancing scarcity with customer demand.
* **Category Performance:** Footwear and limited-edition apparel record the fastest sell-through velocities, requiring tighter safety stock buffers.
* **Fulfillment Efficiency:** Regional distribution nodes maintain strong dispatch SLA compliance rates despite high-volume transaction surges.

---

## 🛠️ Strategic Solutions & Recommendations
1. **Velocity-Based Safety Stock Allocation:** Implement predictive models to adjust safety stock levels dynamically prior to major product drops based on wishlist additions and cart activity.
2. **Multi-Echelon Inventory Rebalancing:** Deploy real-time stock redistribution across regional hubs to prevent regional stockouts.
3. **Agile Rapid Replenishment:** Secure flexible manufacturing capacity with key vendors to enable fast restocks on core high-demand streetwear staples.
